About Max Atelier

Max Atelier is a curated online art marketplace connecting collectors with authentic artwork from emerging and established artists around the world.

Every listing is presented with gallery-level care — verified authenticity, artist profiles, curated exhibitions, and immersive virtual gallery experiences designed to feel intentional, elegant, and trustworthy.

Our Mission

To make discovering, collecting, and commissioning art feel as refined online as it does in a physical gallery.

For Collectors

Browse the marketplace, explore exhibitions, verify certificates, and connect directly with artists.

For Artists

Present your portfolio, manage inquiries, accept custom commissions, and curate your own exhibitions.
